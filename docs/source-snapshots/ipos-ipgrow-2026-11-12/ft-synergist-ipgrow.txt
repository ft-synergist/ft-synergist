Overview
FT Synergist is the Scaling Up Partners for SMEs.
Founded by entrepreneurs for entrepreneurs, we provide hands-on, agile support that goes beyond traditional consulting. Since 2015, we’ve helped over 500 SMEs transform their IP and brand into monetizable assets, expanding locally and overseas with speed and confidence.
Our core services include business strategy development, digital transformation, and franchise growth—all streamlined through our proprietary platforms: Beyond Borders®, a franchise management system that accelerates local and international scaling, and BeyondClassroom®, designed to upskill and future-proof teams so SMEs can outperform competitors.
We also embed sustainability practices and AI-driven insights into our frameworks, ensuring SMEs scale smarter, faster, and more sustainably. With FT Synergist, scaling isn’t guesswork—it’s a proven, predictable path to market leadership.
