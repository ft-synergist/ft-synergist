About this programme
The EDGE Grant covers 8 business areas, and over 150 activities to help your business grow. Whether you're looking to automate your operations, adopt new digital solutions, expand overseas, or build a more sustainable business, EDGE brings all that support under one roof.
Start from what matters most, select the business area most relevant to your priorities: Automation & Digitalisation, Business Strategy, Financial Management, Innovation, Internationalisation, Standards, or Sustainability.

What you need to know
-
Receive up to 70% for SMEs and up to 50% for non-SMEs
Support levels differ by activity. Refer to each activity for more details.
-
Access up to S$100,000 in total grant support per year, across all activities
Use the funding where it matters most. The total grant support cap is shared across all activities and refreshed on 1 April each year.
-
Grant support is disbursed on a reimbursement basis
Claims can be submitted once the activity is completed and full payment has been made.
Are the EDG, MRA and PSG schemes still available?
EDG, MRA and PSG ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September onwards, apply for business grant support under the EDGE Grant.
If you have ongoing EDG, MRA or PSG submissions or projects, these will continue to be processed and you may submit your claims upon project completion.
