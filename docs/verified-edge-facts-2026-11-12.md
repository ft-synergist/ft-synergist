## A. Re-confirmed programme-level facts (main page + FAQ, 12 Nov 2026)

| Fact | Official wording |
|---|---|
| Headline rate | "Receive up to 70% for SMEs and up to 50% for non-SMEs. Support levels differ by activity. Refer to each activity for more details." |
| Detailed split (FAQ) | "up to 50% support for SMEs and up to 30% support for non-SMEs (up to 70% support for internationalisation and sustainability activities for SMEs and up to 50% support for non-SMEs)" |
| Annual cap | S$100,000 per company per year "across all activities"; refreshed 1 April (period 1 Apr – 31 Mar). |
| Digital/automation sub-cap | Up to S$30,000 within the S$100,000 for single-function digital solutions, integrated enterprise systems and pre-scoped equipment. |
| Scope | "8 business areas, and over 150 activities" |
| Legacy schemes | "EDG, MRA and PSG ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September onwards, apply for business grant support under the EDGE Grant." |
| EDGE not an acronym | "EDGE is not an acronym. EDGE conveys the intent of the support, which is for our enterprises to have an edge over their competitors." |
| Eligibility | Registered **and operating** in Singapore; ≥30% ownership (direct or indirect) by Singaporean(s)/PR(s), "determined by the ultimate individual ownership"; no work started or payment/deposit made before application. |
| SME definition | Group annual sales turnover ≤ S$100 million, or group employment ≤ 200 workers. |
| Consultant standard | SS 680 certification (SAC-accredited). "**Examples** of SAC-accredited personnel certification bodies include the Institute of Management Consultants (Singapore), Singapore Business Advisors and Consultants Council Ltd and TUV SUD PSB Pte Ltd." |
| Consultant sourcing | "There is no restriction on where consultants are sourced from, as long as they meet the qualifications required for the grant activity." |
| Related parties | Vendors must not have "any relationship, connection, association or dealings with the grant applicant or its related companies or their directors and common shareholders." |
| Who applies | Applicant only (BGP Preparer/Acceptor). "Third-party companies are not permitted to apply for or manage the grant on behalf of the applicant company." |
| Claims open | 1 Dec 2026 (single-function digital solutions, pre-scoped equipment); 1 Jan 2027 (all other activities). Single and final claim only. |
| Disbursement | Reimbursement only. PayNow Corporate: within 14 working days of claim approval. GIRO: up to 8 weeks. |
| Change requests | Only project end date and claim due date. Any other change: terminate and reapply. |
| Internationalisation | One application per activity per market; multiple markets can run at the same time; DTDi may be claimed on the same expenses, net of the grant. |

## B. LOCKED activity-level facts by business area (verbatim rate lines)

### B1. Business Strategy — tab `#tab=Business+Strategy` (2 activities)
| Activity | Rate (verbatim) | Outcome | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business Strategic Planning** | "Up to 50% support for SMEs and up to 30% support for non-SMEs" | 10 weeks | About: "Formulate strategies and processes to improve your business development, better manage your **intellectual property assets** and optimise operations." Supported: professional service costs. Not supported: regulatory compliance/SOP updates, GST. Complete within 12 months. **Requires vendor's SS 680 certification.** Deliverable: business strategy report (internal analysis, external analysis incl. market size and competitor analysis, growth strategies incl. business models and roadmaps, implementation recommendations). Projections: revenue growth (1 yr) and new jobs paying > S$5,000/month (1 yr). |
| **Business Transition Planning** | None published | — | "Details are still being finalised. We seek your understanding that any submission will not be considered at this time." |

### B2. Internationalisation — tab `#tab=Internationalisation` (7 activities, all "Up to 70% support for SMEs and up to 50% support for non-SMEs")
| Activity | Outcome | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **International Branding** | 10 weeks | "Supports international branding initiatives to facilitate expansion into overseas markets." Areas (choose ≥1): **brand research and analysis; development of brand strategy; development or refresh of brand identity.** Not supported: "Activities aimed at generating domestic business opportunities"; "Operational expenditure such as collateral production, website development, social media management, implementation of brand development roadmap, marketing plans or campaigns". **Requires SS 680**, plus an outline of the overseas market strategy and expansion plan. Deliverable report must show "how the completed project supported the company's overseas expansion plans". 12 months. |
| **International Financial and Tax Planning** | 10 weeks | Requires SS 680. Foreign tax, capital structure and tax strategy report. 12 months. |
| **Identification of Potential Overseas Partnerships** | 6 weeks | Partners named include "licensees, **franchisees**, agents, distributors, suppliers, joint venture partners, and logistics providers". In-person one-to-one meetings at business premises (not events). Not supported: travel, investor sourcing, sourcing of vendors. Vendor must show a track record in the target market (no SS 680 line). |
| **Market Entry Setup** | 6 weeks | Areas: drafting of agreements; entity incorporation (applicant must own > 50%, or the legal maximum); import/export licence; **Intellectual Property** (registration; ownership or exclusive licence must be shown); tax advisory; trade credit insurance. **18 months.** Vendor track record required (no SS 680 line). Generic or template agreements are not supported. One application per market (FAQ). |
| **Overseas Business Development Staff Deployment** | 6 weeks | Basic salary and office rental, both capped at 12 months. SMEs: 70% if a Singaporean/PR is deployed, 50% if a foreigner. Non-SMEs: 50% / 30%. Project must run at least 6 months. Staff cannot be shareholders or directors. |
| **Overseas Marketing & Public Relations** | 6 weeks | In-store promotions, roadshows, pop-ups, social/online/offline media in the target market. Content must be adapted to the market (not translated). Promoted products must be owned or officially represented by the applicant (≥ 50%). |
| **Overseas Physical Trade Fairs** | 6 weeks | Booth rental and build (capped at 36 sqm), speaking slots, collaterals. Not supported: LEAD-supported fairs, fairs open to the public or domestic-only. Apply no earlier than 6 months before the fair. |
