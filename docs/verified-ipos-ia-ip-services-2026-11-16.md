## C. Positioning rules (what FT Synergist may and may not claim)
1. **Claim only the 4 listed services**, using their exact names: IP Strategy Consulting, IP Brokerage and Licensing, Innovation Consultancy, Branding Strategy. Write "and", never "&", in the service names.
2. The other 16 services may be **referenced as part of the IPOS taxonomy or as referral partners**, never as FT Synergist services. This includes valuation, audit, due diligence, agent filing, FTO, contract drafting, dispute resolution and training.
3. Describe the relationship as **"listed on IPOS GoBusiness IP Grow"** or **"IP Grow service provider"**. Never write "IPOS-accredited", "IPOS-certified", "IPOS-approved" or "IPOS-endorsed".
4. **Franchise bridge (permitted, verbatim-backed):** IPOS classes F&B franchising as a form of IP licensing under IP Brokerage and Licensing.
5. **"Singapore as a regional IP hub" appears in NEITHER S.O.T.** It cannot be presented as IPOS wording. Publishing it requires a separately locked primary source.
