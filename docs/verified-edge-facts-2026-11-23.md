## B. LOCKED verbatim wording (copy, metadata, schema and AI answers must use this)

### B1. Programme
- "The EDGE Grant covers 8 business areas, and over 150 activities to help your business grow."
- "Whether you're looking to automate your operations, adopt new digital solutions, expand overseas, or build a more sustainable business, EDGE brings all that support under one roof."
- Business areas, as the 7 website tabs: **Automation & Digitalisation, Business Strategy, Financial Management, Innovation, Internationalisation, Standards, Sustainability.** Automation and Digitalisation count as 2 of the 8 areas.
- "Receive up to 70% for SMEs and up to 50% for non-SMEs: Support levels differ by activity. Refer to each activity for more details."
- "Access up to S$100,000 in total grant support per year, across all activities … The total grant support cap is shared across all activities and refreshed on 1 April each year."
- "Grant support is disbursed on a reimbursement basis: Claims can be submitted once the activity is completed and full payment has been made."

### B3. Business Strategic Planning — full activity terms
- Support level: "Up to 50% support for SMEs and up to 30% support for non-SMEs". Outcome in 10 weeks.
- "Support received for this activity counts towards your company's overall annual grant cap of S$100,000."
- "Project must be completed within 12 months from date of approval."
- "Costs incurred from date of application are covered upon approval."
- Supported: "Professional service costs". Not supported (not limited to): "Expenses relating to regulatory compliance and updates to Standard Operating Procedures"; "Goods and Services Tax (GST)".
- Documents: "Vendor's proposal, quotation, and SS 680 certification"; 1-year revenue-growth projection; 1-year projection of new jobs (overall and Singaporeans only) with gross monthly salary above S$5,000.

### B4. Application process (8 steps, verbatim headings)
1. Prepare your documents
2. Identify a suitable vendor
3. Apply on the Business Grants Portal (BGP) — sign in with Corppass. **"Third-party companies are not permitted to apply for or manage the grant on behalf of the applicant company."**
4. Receive your Letter of Offer (conditions of support, grant amount awarded, project qualifying period, project deliverables)
5. Complete your project. Only two change types are permitted: change to project end date, and change to claim due date.
6. Prepare your claim submission (6a set up your payment method; 6b prepare your claim submission)
7. Submit your claim on BGP. **"Grant eligibility criteria apply until claim submission."**
8. Receive your disbursement. "PayNow Corporate: 14 working days after claim approval"; "GIRO: Up to eight weeks after claim approval".

## E. Rates (combined from the 12 Nov FAQ and activity lock; unchanged)
- General activities: **up to 50% for SMEs and up to 30% for non-SMEs.**
- Internationalisation and Sustainability activities: **up to 70% for SMEs and up to 50% for non-SMEs.**
- Never write a bare "70%" for Business Strategy, Financial Management, Innovation, Large-scale Automation or Large-scale Digital Transformation.
- Single-function Digital Solutions, Integrated Enterprise Systems, Pre-scoped Equipment and Standards: rate **not locked**. Do not publish a percentage.
