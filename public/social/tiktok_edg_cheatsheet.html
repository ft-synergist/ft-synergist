EnterpriseSG EDG Grant:
4 Must-Know Checkpoints

Before submitting your S$30k–S$100k project proposal, verify these 4 filters:

1

30% Local Equity Rule

Entity must be registered & operating in Singapore with minimum 30% local shareholding held by Singaporeans or PRs.

2

Financial Viability Window

Must demonstrate a viable 3-year financial track record or audited accounts showing the financial capacity to complete the project before reimbursement.

3

Qualifying Project Pillars

Covers Core Capabilities (Strategy/HR/Brand), Innovation & Productivity (AI/Automation), or Market Access (MRA & Regional Franchising).

!

Top Rejection Red Flag

Off-the-shelf software or pre-signed vendor contracts are strictly ineligible. Projects must involve qualified certified consultants (SCMC) prior to commencement.
